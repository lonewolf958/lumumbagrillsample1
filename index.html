About Us

Good Food.

Lumumba Grill serves barbecue, chicken, beef, seafood, pizza, pasta and more, at the corner of Lumumba Street and Aggrey Street in Dar es Salaam.

Come hungry and try the Lumumba Mandi, the Lumumba Seafood Plate, or the Lumumba Special Whole Chicken served with rice.

Please allow 30 minutes for your food to be prepared.
